FINAL SCIENTIFIC CAUTIONS
-------------------------
1. Synchronization ±1.5s is exploratory only and excluded from the primary final protocol.
2. Ground localization MAE is conditional on successful matches and must be interpreted with Recall/F1.
3. Dwell MAE is conditional on matched complete visits; Visit Recall/F1 must accompany the error metric.
4. Total transition count can appear accurate while directed flow composition is incorrect; flow matrix metrics are required.
5. WILDTRACK Zone A/B/C are generic spatial regions and must not be labeled as airport functional areas.
